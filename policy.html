MedAlert helps users manage appointments, reminders, and personal health notes. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, MedAlert may store account and profile details (such as your name and phone number), appointment information, reminders, symptom reports, and health tool entries you choose to add, such as allergies, blood type, weight, blood pressure, glucose, and health notes. Do not enter information you do not want associated with your account.

How information is used

Your information is used to provide app features, show your saved records when you sign in, manage appointments and reminders, and maintain the security and operation of the service. Health entries are personal notes and are not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.

Storage and service providers

MedAlert uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud data storage. Information is associated with the signed-in account and is subject to the access controls configured for the service. Supabase may process data as a service provider under its own terms and privacy practices. The app may also use device notification and location features where you choose to use those features; location is used to support nearby health facility lookup.

Sharing

MedAlert does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, or disclosed where required by law or to protect the safety, rights, and security of users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Your records are retained in the service while associated with your account or as needed to operate the app. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account information, contact [your contact email]. Account deletion and backup retention depend on the service configuration and may require processing time.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your sign-in credentials private and sign out on shared devices.

Children

MedAlert is not intended for children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information may contact us to request its removal.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version and its update date will be posted at this page.
